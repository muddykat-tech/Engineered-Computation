Ordering

The Floppy Disk Terminal shows everything your network can craft. Items you have none of are shown faded: click one to order it. To order more of something you already have, Ctrl-click or middle-click it.

Choose how many and the order screen shows what it needs: what comes from storage, what gets crafted on the way, and anything missing. If one recipe needs something another pattern makes, that gets crafted first. Patterns with a tag show with a #: ordering one, pick what to send for each tag.

While an order runs, every terminal on the network shows 102 Processing. The list button beside the terminal's help shows each order, and lets you cancel it.
