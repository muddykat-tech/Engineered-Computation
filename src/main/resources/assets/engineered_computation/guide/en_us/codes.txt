Error Codes

102: A crafting order is running, or carriers are on their way.
200: Moving items.
204: Storage is empty.
404: Nothing matches the search or filter.
409: Mixed Hardware on one network.
413: Network is over 4,096 blocks.
424: Something it needs isn't on the network, likely a processor or a Crafting Computer. A teletype needs a Receiving Station against it.
503: No cells, cable or inventory. For a teletype, no Tabulator Machine on its tubes.
507: No room for the items.
