Crafting Computer

The Crafting Computer runs crafting orders on the network. Build it from Floppy Disk Era casings and form it with the Assembly Wrench. Connect it with cable at its network port, low on its back.

It has eight pattern bays on its front, four down each side. To insert a direct Pattern Floppy, sneak while right-clicking a bay with it. To remove it, sneak while right-clicking the bay with an empty hand. It crafts direct patterns itself, and runs abstract patterns through Advanced Data Processors on the same network.

Right-click it to see its bays, numbered as on the machine, and its screen: its current activity, and the crafting tree of each running order.

It runs up to eight orders at once, abstract orders included. Add more Crafting Computers to run more orders. Breaking a Crafting Computer while it's running will return each order's items to storage where possible, otherwise drop them in the world.
