Compact Floppy Bay

The Compact Floppy Bay holds four 3½-inch floppies. It needs a floppy era processor on the network, such as the Floppy Disk Processor or Floppy Disk Workstation.

Sneak-right-click a bay with a disk to put it in, or with an empty hand to take it out. Right-click elsewhere to open its slots.
