Using a Terminal

Left-click an item to take a stack, right-click to take half, and shift-click to move a stack to your inventory.

To store items, click the grid while holding them (right-click will store one), or shift-click them in your inventory. The bar under the grid shows how full the cells are.

Later terminals add features. The Typewriter shows the grid only. The Uniscope adds a search bar. The Video Terminal and Floppy Disk Terminal add a crafting grid, and the Floppy Disk Terminal has its own internal storage.
