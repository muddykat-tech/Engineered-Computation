Using a Terminal

While in the GUI, you can use Left-click on an item to take a stack, right-click to take half, and shift-click to send a stack to your inventory.

Click with items held to store them (right-click stores one), or shift-click items in your inventory. The bar under the grid shows how full your cells are.

Terminal can be upgraded and have more features added. The Typewriter shows the grid alone. The Uniscope adds a search bar. The Video Terminal and Floppy Disk Terminal add a crafting grid, and the floppy disk termianl has it's own internal storage area.
