Teletype Terminal

The teletype is where items are requested and put away. To connect it to a tube network, place it against a Receiving Station.

Click an item to add a stack to the request slip under the grid, or right-click to add one. Click a line on the slip to remove it, or right-click it for one fewer. When the slip is ready, press the send button beside it and the stations holding each item send them through the tubes. Anything that can't be sent stays on the slip.

"To:" under the slip sets the destination. Click it for a searchable list of every Receiving Station on the tube network, or scroll over it to step through them.

To put items away, click them onto the grid, or shift-click them in your inventory. They go through the tubes to a station that already holds that item, otherwise to one set to take new items. If no station will take them, they won't be sent.

Items whose stations can't be reached are greyed out and show their last tally. While carriers are travelling, the teletype shows 102.
