Teletype Terminal

The teletype is where you request for things and put them away. Place it against a Receiving Station to connect to a tube network.

Click an item to put a stack on the request slip under the grid, or right-click to add one. Click a line on the slip to strike it off, or right-click it for one fewer. When the slip is ready, press the send button beside it and the stations holding each item send these items through the tubes. Anything that would fail to send will stays on the slip bar.

"To:" under the slip is where things are sent. Click it to show a drop down list of every Receiving Station on the same tube network, allows the ability to search them by name. You can also scroll over it to step through them. Name your Receiving Stations to find them easily.

To put something away, click it onto the grid, or shift-click it in your inventory. It goes through the tubes to a station that already lists it as an item, or failing that to one set to take new items. If nowhere will take it, it will fail to send.

Items whose stations can't be reached right now are greyed out, showing their last tally. While carriers are on their way, the teletype shows 102.
