Analogue Era

Relays, punch cards and pneumatic tubes.

In this era, all items are still stored in generic chests or other inventories, however, these can be connected via Tube Stations and a network of Pneumatic Tubes linked via a Tabulator Machine and a Teletype Typewriter to create a central storage hub and network.

The Tabulator Machine's holds Card Decks comprised of set Punch Cards, these cards are used to mark the location of a Tube Station to the network.
